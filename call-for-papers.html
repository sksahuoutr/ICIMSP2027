Invitation

The School of Electronic Sciences, OUTR invites original research contributions for the International Conference on Intelligent Machine and Signal Processing (ICIMSP 2027), to be held in Hybrid format at Bhubaneswar from 1–3 July 2027.

ICIMSP 2027 focuses on innovation at the intersection of Intelligent Machines, Autonomous Systems, and Advanced Signal & Image Processing.

Proceedings Publication: Submitted a proposal to publish the ICIMSP 2027 proceedings with Springer Nature Group. (Subject to final approval.)

Conference Tracks

Track 1: Intelligent Systems & Autonomous Technologies

Topics
  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems
  • Intelligent Control and Automation
  • Human–Machine and Brain–Computer Interaction
  • Cyber-Physical Systems and Industry 4.0
  • Smart Manufacturing and Digital Twins
  • IoT and Industrial IoT (IIoT)
  • Embedded Control and Real-Time Intelligent Systems
  • Robust and Adversarial Intelligent Systems

Focus: Intelligent autonomous technologies integrating robotics, real-time embedded control, industrial automation, IoT-enabled systems, and human-centered intelligent applications.

Track 2: Signal Processing & Computer Vision

Topics
  • Signal, Image, and Video Processing
  • Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition
  • Image Enhancement and Restoration
  • Scene Understanding and Motion Analysis
  • Biometrics and Intelligent Perception Systems
  • Medical Imaging and Healthcare Applications
  • Remote Sensing and Hyperspectral Imaging
  • Vision Systems for Autonomous Applications
  • Edge and Embedded Vision Processing

Focus: Theoretical and applied research in signal processing, computer vision, visual analytics, and perception-oriented imaging technologies for real-world applications.

Track 3: Artificial Intelligence, Algorithms & Intelligent Architectures

Topics
  • Computational Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning and Explainable AI
  • Optimization and Evolutionary Algorithms
  • Data Intelligence and Intelligent Decision Systems
  • Intelligent Communication and Network Security
  • Edge, Cloud, and Distributed Computing
  • AI Hardware, VLSI, and Embedded AI Systems
  • Smart Sensors and Emerging Intelligent Technologies
  • Blockchain-enabled Intelligent Applications

Focus: Advanced AI methodologies, intelligent computing paradigms, secure communication technologies, AI-specific hardware architectures, and emerging intelligent applications.

Student Paper Contest

  • B.E./B.Tech/M.Sc Poster: 1st ₹5,000, 2nd ₹3,000
